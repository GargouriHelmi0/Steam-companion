How do I find my Steam ID?

How to Find Your Steam ID

1

Open Steam and click your profile name in the top menu bar.

2

Select "View Profile" from the dropdown.

3

Right-click anywhere on the page and select "Copy Page URL".

4

Paste the URL here — it will look like one of these:

https://steamcommunity.com/id/yourname https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198...

If your URL uses a custom name, we will look up the numeric ID for you automatically.
